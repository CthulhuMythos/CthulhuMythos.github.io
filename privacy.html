Privacy Policy

rclone-JWZR is used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google Drive. It does not collect, store, share or sell any data from other users. Access tokens stay in the owner's private environment and can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings.

隐私政策

本应用仅供所有者本人访问其自己的 Google Drive，不收集、存储、分享或出售任何其他用户的数据。授权令牌只保存在所有者的私有环境中，可随时在 Google 账号设置中撤销。
